Breach 11 — Open redirect

Flag: none (vulnerability without a flag). Type: OWASP A01 — Broken Access Control (Unvalidated Redirect)

How it works

/redirect?next=<url> issues a 307 to whatever next contains, with no allow-list — including external origins (https://evil.example.com), scheme-relative (//evil.example.com) and even javascript: URLs. The footer and the Google login button route through it (/redirect?next=https://accounts.google.com), so links look trustworthy.

Impact

Phishing (a 42.tech link that lands on an attacker site), and theft of tokens/codes appended to a legitimate-looking redirect (e.g. OAuth flows).

Remediation

  • Redirect only to a server-side allow-list of known paths/hosts, or to

relative paths you control; reject absolute/scheme-relative/javascript: URLs.

  • If an external redirect is unavoidable, show an interstitial warning.
